# Privacy

`joshapproved.com` is a static marketing site. It serves HTML, CSS, fonts, and SVG assets. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no ad network, and no cookie banner because there are no cookies to ask about.

## What we collect

Nothing. The site runs no JavaScript that talks to a server we control.

## What our host sees

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare logs incoming requests at the edge (IP, user-agent, URL) for abuse mitigation and DDoS protection. We do not look at those logs and do not use them for advertising or product analytics. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for their retention defaults.

## What individual apps collect

Each app linked from this site has its own privacy policy in its own repository. Most collect nothing. The badge on the listing tells you what the app talks to, if anything.

## Contact

If you spot a privacy issue with the site, email `info@joshapproved.com`.
